FAQ

Common questions.

Is VRBOT safe? Will I get banned? +
VRBOT uses human-like delays, randomized task ordering, session breaks, and never fires taps in rapid succession. It mimics real player behavior as closely as possible. No bot is 100% risk-free, but VRBOT is built with anti-ban as a first-class requirement.
How quickly can I get started? +
Server plan accounts are provisioned within minutes. You'll receive login details and the dashboard URL. Total setup time is around 2 minutes — no technical knowledge required.
Can I control which tasks run? +
Yes. Each task can be enabled or disabled individually through the config. You can also control priority levels, cooldown intervals, and task-specific settings like resource priorities or troop types.
What's the difference between PC and Server plans? +
The PC plan runs on your own computer via LDPlayer emulator — cheaper but requires your PC to stay on. The Server plan runs 24/7 in the cloud on Hetzner — no hardware needed, always on, accessible from your phone.
Do you offer refunds? +
Contact us on Telegram within 24 hours of purchase if the bot is not working for your account and we'll make it right. We want every customer running smoothly.
